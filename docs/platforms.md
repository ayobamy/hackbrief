# Hackathon platforms

Every platform found that lists hackathons or competitions, grouped by kind and region, with what
each shows and what hackr may do with it. Researched on 2 November 2026. The verdicts and their
reasons are in [sources.md](sources.md):

- **Use now:** an API, feed or open licence allows it.
- **With care:** allowed, with conditions to settle first.
- **Ask:** link out until the site agrees.
- **Link out:** store what a user enters and link to it; collect nothing.
- **Do not collect.**

"Rules" means the platform shows each event's rules or judging criteria; "Winners" means it shows
past winning projects, which feed hackr's past-winner patterns.

## Africa

No single African hackathon calendar or government portal was found: events are announced one at
a time, by communities, companies and agencies. That makes organiser submissions (route D in
START-HERE) and links shared by users the main way to cover the region.

| Source | What it lists | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Zindi](https://zindi.africa) | African data-science competitions | Ask |
| [tech.africa events](https://tech.africa/events/) | Pan-African tech events, few hackathons | Link out; its legal page allows automated access at under one request a second within robots.txt, but event data is not openly licensed |
| [dev.events Africa](https://dev.events) | Tech events by region, including Lagos | Ask |
| [Opportunity Desk](https://opportunitydesk.org) | Opportunity posts, many Africa-tagged hackathons | Ask |

| Government and agency challenge pages | Single events, such as national e-government hackathons | Link out, added by hand |

## Gaps

- Terms that need a browser to read: Kaggle, Unstop, Hack2skill, Allevents, Junction, Meetup,
  AKINDO.
- Thin coverage of Latin America and Asia beyond Japan.
- None of the platforms read publishes judging weights as data; they appear only in each event's
  rules text, which is why the coach reads pasted rules.
