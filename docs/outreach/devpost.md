# Request to Devpost

A draft for Ahmed to send to `support@devpost.com`, the contact in section 20 of Devpost's
[Terms of Service](https://info.devpost.com/terms). Devpost's terms forbid scraping (section 4) and
reuse of Devpost content without written consent (section 7A), so hackbrief does not collect from
Devpost until this is answered ([sources.md](../sources.md)). Record the reply there.

---

**Subject:** Permission request: hackathon listings for hackbrief, an open-source hackathon guide for
students

Hello Devpost team,

I maintain hackbrief (<https://github.com/ayobamy/hackbrief>), a free, open-source (MIT) tool that helps
students and early-career developers choose hackathons and prepare for them. Its first feature
reads a hackathon's rules and turns the judging criteria into a plan; you can try it at
<https://hackbrief.dev/coach>. It is non-commercial.

Many of the hackathons our users want are on Devpost, and your Terms of Service rule out
collecting them without written consent, so I am asking first. We would like to show, for public
hackathons on Devpost:

- the name, dates, location or online format, and prize amounts;
- the judging criteria and their weights, as published in the rules;
- for past hackathons, the names of winning projects and links to their Devpost pages;

each with a prominent link back to the hackathon's Devpost page as the source.

We would not copy descriptions, collect user profiles or personal data, or use the data
commercially. If Devpost has an API, feed, export or partner programme for this, we would gladly
use it instead; otherwise we would follow whatever limits you set on what we show, how often we
fetch it and how we credit Devpost.

Would Devpost allow this, or is there a better route? I am happy to share more detail or a demo.

Kind regards,
Ahmed Olawale
Maintainer, hackbrief
<https://github.com/ayobamy/hackbrief>
