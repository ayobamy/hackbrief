# Request to Kaggle

A draft for Ahmed to send to `support@kaggle.com`, the support address in Kaggle's
[Terms of Use](https://www.kaggle.com/terms) (effective 22 June 2025). The terms allow only "your
own internal, personal, non-commercial use, and not on behalf of or for the benefit of any third
party", and bar use that "Copies or stores any significant portion of the Content". That covers the
official API as well as the site, so hackbrief does not list Kaggle competitions until this is
answered ([sources.md](../sources.md), "Ask permission"). Record the reply there.

---

**Subject:** Permission request: listing Kaggle competitions in hackbrief, an open-source guide for
students

Hello Kaggle team,

I maintain hackbrief (<https://github.com/ayobamy/hackbrief>), a free, open-source (MIT) tool that
helps students and early-career developers choose hackathons and competitions and prepare for them.
It lists upcoming events at <https://hackbrief.dev/events> and turns an event's rules into a plan at
<https://hackbrief.dev/coach>. It is non-commercial.

Kaggle competitions are a natural fit for our users, and your Terms of Use limit use of the
Services, the API included, to personal, non-commercial use, so I am asking first. We would like
to read, once a day through the official Kaggle API, for public competitions that are open to
entry:

- the title, start date and entry deadline;
- the category and the reward as Kaggle states it;
- the link to the competition's page on Kaggle;

and show them in our list with a prominent link back to the competition on Kaggle as the source.

We would not copy descriptions, rules, datasets, notebooks or leaderboards, collect anything about
Kaggle users, or use the data commercially, and we would keep only the current list rather than an
archive. If Kaggle prefers a different route, such as a feed, a partner programme or limits on what
we show, how often we read it and how we credit Kaggle, we would follow it.

Would Kaggle allow this? I am happy to share more detail or a demo.

Kind regards,
Ahmed Olawale
Maintainer, hackbrief
<https://github.com/ayobamy/hackbrief>
